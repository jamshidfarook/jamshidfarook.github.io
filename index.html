About Me

A little bit about me

I got into data analytics because I genuinely enjoy the puzzle of it, taking a spreadsheet full of chaos and finding the story hiding inside. I've worked on everything from cleaning up messy HR datasets to building sales dashboards from scratch in Power BI.

I picked up my Google and IBM certifications along the way, but honestly the best learning happened while actually building things. I work with clients on Upwork and Fiverr, mostly on dashboards, reporting, and making sense of data that's been sitting around unused.
